Everything You Need to Get Digging

From finding local trail work days to organizing volunteers and managing tools — Trail Dig Days makes trail stewardship simple.

Find Events Near You

Browse trail building and maintenance events on an interactive map. Filter by date, location, and activity type to find the perfect dig day.

Organize Volunteers

Create events, track RSVPs, and communicate with volunteers. Know exactly who's coming and what tools they're bringing.

Track Tools & Gear

List what you're providing and what volunteers should bring. Manage tool inventory and ensure everyone comes prepared.

How It Works

Whether you're a seasoned trail steward or a first-time volunteer, getting involved is easy.

1

Create an Account

Sign up as a volunteer or organization. Set your preferences and tell us where you want to dig.

2

Find or Create Events

Browse the map for upcoming trail work days or create your own if you're an organization.

3

Show Up & Dig

RSVP, bring your gear, and join the community in building and maintaining the trails we all love.

Who It's For

Trail Dig Days brings together everyone who cares about great trails.

🛠️ Volunteers

  • Discover trail work days near you
  • RSVP and track your volunteer hours
  • Get notified about new events in your area
  • Connect with local trail organizations
  • Join a community of trail stewards

🏞️ Organizations

  • Create and manage dig day events
  • Track volunteer registrations
  • List provided tools and gear needs
  • Communicate with volunteers
  • Build your local trail community
